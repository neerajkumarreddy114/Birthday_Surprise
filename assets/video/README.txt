TO USE YOUR OWN SURPRISE VIDEO
1. Copy your video into this folder (assets/video/), e.g. my-surprise.mp4  (MP4 / H.264 works on every phone)
2. Open index.html, find the line marked "CHANGE VIDEO HERE" and change
   src="assets/video/surprise.mp4"  ->  src="assets/video/my-surprise.mp4"
   (or just replace surprise.mp4 with your file, keeping the same name)
surprise.mp4 is only a 2-second temporary placeholder.
Music: assets/music/background-music.mp3
Name / passcode / letter: top of js/script.js
